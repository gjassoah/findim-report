## Conventions and groups

All modules are left T-modules, products act from right to left, and
[1] = Omega^-1. Upper-case letters denote the basis of DC dual to the
lower-case basis `e,x,y,z,u,v,t,j,f,n` of C. Thus F is dual to f, and
is an element of T, not a field symbol in the module formulas below.
Put M_i = Omega^i s, with M_0=s.

For X --f--> Y --g--> Z --h--> W, the ordering is <h,g,f>, with value in
stable Hom(X[1],W). A homogeneous triple of degrees a,b,d consequently
has degree a+b+d-1. The product tau beta means tau[-1] beta in H^2;
beta squared means beta[-1] beta in H^-2. These bookkeeping statements
and the indeterminacy formulas below have status **AI-proved**.

## Explicit representatives

The following argument has status **AI-proved** over F_2(q). The same
formulas are also checked in all three specialisations. Inside the indicated
projective modules the first syzygies have ordered bases

\[
\begin{aligned}
M_1&=\langle u,v,n,T,J,F,N\rangle\subset Tf,\\
M_2&=\langle x+y,z,j,E,X+qY\rangle\subset Te,\\
M_3&=\langle x+qy,z,j,E,X+Y,U,V\rangle\subset Te.
\end{aligned}
\]

The maps onto s, M_1 and M_2 are, respectively, the f-coordinate
projection Tf -> s, right multiplication by u on Te, and right
multiplication by x+y on Te. Their kernels are the displayed spaces.
In particular M_1 is generated by u and M_2 by x+y; both generators
lie at e. Thus Hom(M_1,s)=Hom(M_2,s)=0.

Choose beta_0(1)=F. The solver checks that Hom(s,M_1) is one-dimensional
and its projective-factor subspace is zero. More explicitly, every such
factor lifts through the cover Te -> M_1, whereas Hom(s,Te)=0:
on fTe = <t,j,U,V>, left multiplication by u has the linearly independent
images y+qx, z, E, X+Y. Hence beta_0 is a non-zero stable generator.

Right multiplication by U gives a lift Tf -> Te of beta_0, since Uu=F.
Its restriction is beta_1 = Omega beta_0: M_1 -> M_2, with
beta_1(u)=E and all other displayed basis values zero.
Right multiplication by X lifts beta_1, since X(x+y)=E. Its restriction
beta_2 = Omega^2 beta_0 sends x+y to E and the other M_2 basis vectors
to zero. Choose theta: M_3 -> s by theta(V)=1 and zero on the other basis vectors.
It represents tau[-3].

The functional theta is T-linear. The module M_3 is generated by x+qy and V:
y(x+qy)=z, t(x+qy)=(1+q^3)j, X(x+qy)=E, uV=X+Y and nV=U;
here 1+q^3 is non-zero in every field listed.
Its first generator lies at e, so every
map M_3 -> s is a scalar multiple of theta. No non-zero such map factors
through projectives: any factor extends across M_3 -> Te by injectivity,
and Hom(Te,s)=0. Thus theta represents a generator of H^3.

In these representatives,

\[
\beta_1\beta_0=0,\qquad \theta\beta_2=0,\qquad
\beta_2\beta_1=0
\]

as literal module maps. The first two certify the required vanishing
products in degrees -2 and 2; the last is needed for the beta triple.
The equalities follow from beta_1(F)=0, theta(E)=0 and beta_2(E)=0.

## Cone of tau and the scalar

Set W = <x+qy,z,j,E,X+Y,U> = ker(theta). The pushout cone of
theta: M_3 -> s along the injection M_3 -> P_2=Te is

\[
C=(s\oplus Te)/\{(\theta(m),m):m\in M_3\}\cong Te/W.
\]

The displayed isomorphism sends (lambda,a) to [a+lambda V]. Thus the
triangle and its maps have the concrete form

\[
M_3\xrightarrow{\theta}s\xrightarrow{\iota}C\xrightarrow{p}M_2,
\qquad \iota(1)=[V],\qquad p([a])=a(x+y).
\]

Shifting this triangle by 3 gives the cone triangle of tau: s -> s[3].
The module C has dimension 6, and 0 -> s -> C -> M_2 -> 0 is exact.

Define L: M_1 -> C by L(au)=[aX]. This is well defined: the kernel
of right multiplication by u is M_2, and M_2 X is contained in W
(the only non-zero product on its displayed basis is (x+y)X=E).
Moreover,

\[
pL(au)=aX(x+y)=aE=\beta_1(au),\qquad
L\beta_0(1)=L(F)=L(Uu)=[UX]=0.
\]

The last equality uses (DC)^2=0. Therefore iota c = L beta_0 has solution
c=0. This is the cone-of-the-last-map definition of the bracket for
M_1 --beta_1--> M_2 --beta_2--> M_3 --theta--> s: the lift is of
beta_2[1]=beta_1, and the final composite uses beta_1[1]=beta_0.
It gives a bracket element in Hom(M_1[1],s)=Hom(s,s), as required.

For a homogeneous triple <a,b,d>, the indeterminacy is
a H^(|b|+|d|-1) + H^(|a|+|b|-1) d. Here it is
tau H^-3 + H^1 beta_0 = 0. We already have H^1=0. Also H^-3=0
without taking a quotient: fM_3=<j,U,V>, and left multiplication by u
sends these vectors to the independent vectors z,E,X+Y. Thus there
is no non-zero map s -> M_3. Consequently the bracket is exactly {0}.

## Separate computation of the beta triple

For s --beta_0--> M_1 --beta_1--> M_2 --beta_2--> M_3, use the
injection s -> Tf given by 1 -> F. Its pushout gives

\[
C_\beta=(M_1\oplus Tf)/k(F,F),\qquad \dim C_\beta=14.
\]

The map G: C_beta -> M_2, G([m,a])=beta_1(m), is T-linear and well
defined because beta_1(F)=0. It extends beta_1 from M_1. Since
beta_2 beta_1=0 literally, beta_2 G=0. Hence the zero map
s[1] -> M_3 is a Toda representative. Its degree is -4, because
stable Hom(s[1],M_3)=stable Hom(s,M_4)=H^-4.
The indeterminacy beta_0 H^-3 + H^-3 beta_0 vanishes. This gives
<beta_0,beta_0,beta_0>={0} independently of the tau-cone lift.
No general comparison theorem equating these two different brackets
is needed or claimed.
