| `W_ij(s),U_j(r)` | `n_i=s-r`, `n_j=-r` |
| `W_ij(s),U_h(r)` for the third index | `n_i=s`, `n_h=-r` |
| `W_ij(s),V_i(r)` | `n_i=r`, `n_j=r-s` |
| `W_ij(s),V_h(r)` for the third index | `n_i=s`, `n_h=r` |
| `U_i(r),W_ij(s)` | `n_i=-r`, `n_j=-(r+s)` |
| `W_ij(s),V_j(r)` | `n_i=r+s`, `n_j=r` |

The two noncommuting relations use the required third weight `r+s`.
The `W/U` and `W/V` case splits cover all the permitted indices and
preserve the argument order `s,r` used by the presentation's universal
map. `family_u_sq` conjugates the finite involution relation. Thus,
`fromG : G →* P` supplies exactly every required relation to
`SelectionGroup.lift`.

`toG : P →* G` maps the finite generators to the torus generators and
zero-parameter root generators of `G`. Its kernel-relation checks use
the actual conjugation theorem of `G`: zero weight gives commutation,
and successive conjugation by `T_j` and `T_i` gives the pair relation
for `W_ij`. In `toG_family`, the `U` case uses exponent `-r` and weight
`-1`, whose product is `r`; the `V` and `W` cases use exponent `r` and
weight `1`. This checks the negative sign that is essential to the
parameter reconstruction.

`fromG_comp_toG` is the identity on `P`, while `toG_comp_fromG` is the
identity on `G`. These are supplied in the corresponding order to
`MonoidHom.toMulEquiv`, giving `presentationEquiv : P ≃* G`.
`presentationEquiv_T` and `presentationEquiv_root0` state exactly the
requested generator images. `finitelyPresented` installs finiteness of
the actual relator subtype, applies Mathlib's finite `PresentedGroup`
instance, and transports it in the direction `P → G`.

All parts of Proposition 5.9 are therefore represented by the displayed
declarations, without a fallback finite-presentation predicate or a
changed defining relation. The final five-gate receipt is maintained by
the coordinator; this note makes no claim about its outcome before that
run finishes.
