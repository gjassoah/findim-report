## 1. Proposition 9.14 — does the report's proof close my Phase A gap?

**Verdict: NO ERROR FOUND (one WORDING point). Yes, it closes the gap.**

My Phase A gap: existence of a stable bimodule map g_λ: E_λ → C[3] with nonzero evaluation, which I had reduced
to three obstructions (i) τ̂1ξ = 0, (ii) τ̂2ξ = 0, (iii) a Massey-type secondary obstruction, for a degree −1
class ξ. The report does not use obstruction theory; it writes down an explicit chain map defined below degree 0
("tail" chain map), which is exactly what a stable morphism from a module is. Step-by-step check:

1. Casimir. Σ_w w ⊗ w* (w over the 20-element basis 𝔅, w* trace dual) corresponds to id_T under
   x⊗y ↦ (t ↦ x tr(yt)), hence is central in the bimodule T ⊗_k T; applying h_λ to the first factor gives
   h_λ(a)ξ_λ = ξ_λ a in T ⊗_I T. Correct (by hand; also `check_twisted_lift.out` (C): true for all 20 basis a).
   So b(x) = xξ_λ is a bimodule map T_{h_λ} → 𝔅^0 (right action of T_{h_λ}: x*a = x h_λ(a); b(x h(a)) = xh(a)ξ =
   xξa). Correct.
2. Σ_w h_λ(w)w* = 0: for w ∈ C, ww* and w*w are the duals of the left/right end idempotents; e is the left end
   of 6 and the right end of 6 basis vectors of C, f of 4 and 4; so the sum is 6e* + 4f* + λ(6e* + 4f*) = 0 in
   characteristic 2. Correct. The refined identity Σ_{w radical, left idempotent r} h_λ(w)w* = r* is correct
   (script (A): gives E for r = e and F for r = f).
3. Twisted Casimir identity Σ_w a h_λ(w) ⊗_I w* + Σ_w h_λ(w) ⊗_I w* h_λ^{−1}(a) = a ⊗_I r* (a radical, right
   idempotent r, w radical): obtained from aξ_λ = ξ_λ h_λ^{−1}(a) by dropping the terms with an idempotent first

   generators by ψ(a0[…]a_{n+1}) = a0ψ([…])h_λ^{−1}(a_{n+1}). Correct (this is the bimodule-map rule for the
