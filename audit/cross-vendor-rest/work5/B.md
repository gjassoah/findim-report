## (1) Corollary 5.5(3): GAP (the cited results could not be checked)

The report gives an argument, but it rests entirely on citations:
- R = B_Σ.
- "By a theorem of Schofield [Sch07, Thm 2.3], every universal localisation of B is of the form studied in
  [Sch07]".
- For these, K_0(B^op) → K_0(B_Σ^op) is surjective [Sch07, Lemma 4.1].
- K_0(B^op) is finitely generated by case (1), so K_0(B_Σ^op) is finitely generated.

The deduction from the cited facts is correct: surjectivity plus finite generation gives finite rank,
so χ has finite-dimensional image and Proposition 5.4 applies.

I could not check [Sch07, Thm 2.3] or [Lemma 4.1]. In particular I cannot confirm:
- whether Sch07 needs k algebraically closed;
- which side and which kind of maps Σ consists of (maps between finitely generated projectives);
- that the surjectivity is stated for all universal localisations, or only for those of the special form.

The report does not state the hypotheses of the cited results. "Universal localisation" is not defined in
the statement: is Σ a set of maps between finitely generated projective modules, and on which side? The side
does not matter for K_0, since P ↦ Hom(P,R) identifies the projectives on the two sides.

This is the same type of dependence as in my Phase A, which cited Bergman–Dicks/Schofield for "projectives
over a universal localisation of a hereditary ring are induced". The report needs nothing beyond surjectivity
on K_0, which is weaker. Verdict: GAP (citation unchecked). No error is visible.
