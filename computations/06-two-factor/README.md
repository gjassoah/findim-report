# Codex job 10

Claim: replace the AR source's bar cone complexes by projective complexes
built from minimal one-factor covers, measure the two-factor terms, and
compute the evaluated cone's stable Hom profile. Case: seed 10102026 over
GF(2^16). Conventions: left modules, column maps, homological complexes,
characteristic two. Numerical claims have status **supported** in this case.

The report is [audit/10-two-factor-codex.md](../../audit/10-two-factor-codex.md).
The scripts use the installed system Python/SageMath and the read-only
computation sources in `02-ar-finite-data/` and `05-candidate1/`.
`reuse.py` redirects Sage state to this directory and disables bytecode
writes. It adapts exact matrix multiplication/concatenation as in job 08,
and selects projective generators in the small quotient by the radical.
Every cover still checks surjectivity and all 40 action identities.

Run from the repository root, stopping on a nonzero exit code:

```sh
python3 -B computations/06-two-factor/one_factor.py > computations/06-two-factor/one-factor.out 2>&1
python3 -B computations/06-two-factor/evaluated.py > computations/06-two-factor/evaluated.out 2>&1
python3 -B computations/06-two-factor/validate.py > computations/06-two-factor/validation.out 2>&1
python3 -B computations/06-two-factor/sizes.py > computations/06-two-factor/sizes.out 2>&1
```

`one_factor.py --resume` reuses this job's reconstructed cone and restarts
its projective tail. `evaluated.py --resume` reuses this job's evaluated
two-cone and restarts its Hom calculation. In the recorded run these
commands produced `one-factor-tail-optimized.out` and `profile.out`.
The initial tail calculation and Hom calculation were interrupted to
replace expensive elimination steps by equivalent smaller operations.
The initial `one-factor.out` also records a dense/sparse concatenation
error after the cone was saved; the corrected continuation completed.
The `Timeout (0:01:00)!` lines are periodic stack diagnostics inherited
from job 08, not failed mathematical assertions or a computation deadline.

Artifacts:

- `one-factor.sobj`: the actual cone, transferred cocycle, original covers,
  and five minimal projective terms with their differentials.
- `evaluated.sobj`: an actual A-module representing the evaluated two-cone,
  its quotient maps and tensor-complex differentials.
- `profile-matrices.sobj`: the complete-resolution and Hom differentials.
- The JSON files record parameters, dimensions, ranks, checks, and the
  stopped operation; the `.out` files retain the corresponding runs.

The unknown bound is 2,000,000 **scalar coordinates in one coefficient
system**, independent of its number of right-hand sides. `sizes.py`
checks dimensions before allocating the two-factor bimodule presentation.
It exits normally only after recording the requested bound stop. This is
a stop for this full-space presentation; no lower bound on all alternative
representatives or on cornerwise algorithms is asserted.

The evaluated calculation replaces projective complexes by smaller
projective resolutions **before** their external tensor product. It does
not take the external square of two stable modules. Its profile is a
direct calculation; the fibre, Lambda, Z, delta0, and the requested
ordinary Lambda-Ext calculations remain unperformed after the bound stop.

The SageMath data files (`*.sobj`) written by these scripts are not included in the repository; they are
regenerated by running the scripts. Their SHA-256 checksums, where recorded, are in `SHA256SUMS`.
